^ Caret → allows minor + patch updates. Its means our project automatically updated if any new version comes in 4.x.x series
~ Tilde → allows patch updates only. Its means our project automatically updated if any new patch comes in 4.4.x series
If we don't add the ~ or ^ then its mean that our version will never auto update.
